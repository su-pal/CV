Subrata Pal

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Chemistry at Oklahoma State University, working with Professor Spencer P. Pitre on cobalt-photocatalyzed reactions. My research investigates the design and mechanistic understanding of first-row transition metal (Co) photocatalysts, with an emphasis on their application in carbon–carbon bond-forming reactions for the synthesis of pharmaceutically relevant molecules. I am seeking postdoctoral research positions in photoredox catalysis, or synthetic methodology development, with long-term interests in an independent academic research career.
